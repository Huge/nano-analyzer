Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our credit billing model.

Do prepaid credits expire?

No. Your credits remain in your balance until consumed by scans. If your team has a slow sprint with few PRs, your balance remains untouched.

What happens if a scan fails?

If a scan encounters an infrastructure error or unhandled exception, zero credits are deducted. You only pay for successful, actionable reports.

Can we bring our own LLM keys?

Yes. Self-hosted runners can configure their own API keys (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Vertex AI, or local Ollama/vLLM) with 0% compute markup.

How does --max-delay work?

When approaching the deadline, the budget governor stops scheduling new triage rounds and exports a partial report with files covered, preventing pipeline timeouts.
